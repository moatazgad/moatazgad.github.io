About

I work in cloud and DevOps, which mostly means spending my time making infrastructure invisible in the right way — reliable enough that nobody has to think about it. That covers designing cloud architecture, building CI/CD pipelines, running Kubernetes in production, and making systems observable enough that when something does go wrong, it's obvious why.

What I care about more than any specific tool is the discipline behind good infrastructure — automated over manual, documented over remembered, boring over clever. I like the problems that sit between code and production, and I like handing off systems the next person can actually understand.
