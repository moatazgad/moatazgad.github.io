03

About

I'm a Cloud & DevOps engineer who works across AWS and Azure, building the CI/CD pipelines, container platforms, and infrastructure-as-code that let teams ship reliably. Day to day that means owning Kubernetes and ECS workloads in production, architecting cloud environments with Terraform, and building CI/CD strategy across Jenkins, Azure DevOps, and GitLab CI — usually across multiple environments and teams at once.

I care as much about what happens after deployment as before it: I design logging and observability strategy with the ELK stack — sharding, ILM policies, index templating — so teams can actually see what their systems are doing. I've also managed artifact repositories and Helm chart lifecycles across multi-cluster environments, working closely with cross-functional teams to keep delivery and infrastructure moving together. The common thread is making infrastructure boring in the best sense: predictable, observable, and easy to hand off.
